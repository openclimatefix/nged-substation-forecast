# School-term dates for experiment 11

`lincolnshire_term_dates.csv` holds the first and last day of each of the six terms in Lincolnshire
County Council's recommended school calendar, for the academic years 2021-22 to 2025-26. All 20
series the experiment scores sit inside Lincolnshire (latitude 52.7 to 53.3, longitude -0.2 to
0.33, all in the East Midlands licence area), so one calendar covers them.

| Academic years | Source |
| --- | --- |
| 2021-22, 2022-23, 2023-24 | [LCC "School term times", previous years](https://www.lincolnshire.gov.uk/school-attendance/school-term-times/2), read 2026-10-14 |
| 2024-25 | The 2024/25 "Recommended school terms and holidays" PDF published by [Heckington Primary School](https://www.heckington.lincs.sch.uk/mediaFolder/Term%20Dates%202024-25.pdf), checked against the amendment LCC's [current page](https://www.lincolnshire.gov.uk/school-attendance/school-term-times) states (term 5 starts 23 April 2025, summer half term ends 1 June 2025) |
| 2025-26 | [LCC "School term times"](https://www.lincolnshire.gov.uk/school-attendance/school-term-times), read 2026-10-14, and matching in two school calendars ([Heckington](https://heckington.lincs.sch.uk/mediaFolder/2024-2025/Term%20Dates%202025-26-2025327114344.pdf), [Boston High School](https://www.bostonhighschool.co.uk/key-information/term-dates)) |

**Licence.** The council's web page states "All content © 2026 Lincolnshire County Council" and no
licence. The council's open-data portal lists the same recommended dates (current and next school
year only) under the Open Government Licence v3.0, via
[data.gov.uk](https://www.data.gov.uk/dataset/f9c3e77d-ccf4-4da5-927d-c87a4d5c599e/school-term-dates).
The file records only calendar dates, which are facts, and links to its sources.

**Limits.** The calendar applies to community and voluntary-controlled schools. Academies, voluntary-aided
schools, and foundation schools set their own dates and often differ by a day or two. The 2024-25 year
rests on one school's copy of the council's calendar plus the council's note about its amendment.
An older copy of that calendar (Olive Anderson School) gives term 5 starting 22 April and term 6
starting 3 June 2025, which the council's amendment later replaced. Years before 2021-22 are not
collected, so the experiment trains every arm from 2021-09-01.
