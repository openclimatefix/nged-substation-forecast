# Metered solar farms

**The summary of this theme is in progress.** This theme covers the six solar farms whose output NGED
meters in the trial area (labelled A to F). The experiments ask what shape each farm's output has,
what physical plant (panel tilt, azimuth, ratio of direct-current to alternating-current capacity,
heat loss, and inverter clip) reproduces it, and what that implies for the unmetered solar behind
Primary substations. The [Experiments](experiments.md) page lists every experiment tried, and the
[Research plan](plan.md) lists what is planned next.
