# Differentiable physics: long history and drift

**The first experiment of this theme shows that the data lets the series go ahead, with three
changes: cut the first months of most Primary substation series, fix the lockdown window in advance,
and count eligible long-history sites at each tuning year's first day.** This theme covers whether a
differentiable-physics model of Primary substation demand can use old years of history and cope with
drift in the demand level over the years. The [Experiments](experiments.md) page lists every
experiment tried, with its verdict and a take-home message, and the [Research plan](plan.md) lists
what is planned next.

## What the first experiment found

- **[00103 - Differentiable physics, part
  1](../../00103-differentiable-physics-1-data-look/result.md):** 13 of the 16 Primaries need
  leading months cut (19.4 months at D4), the data does not isolate the 2020 lockdown from drift, and
  the power timestamps agree with the sun to within about 5 minutes on both sides of NGED's fix of
  2026-03-26.
