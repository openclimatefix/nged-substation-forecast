# Differentiable physics: demand, calendar, and weather

**The summary of this theme is in progress.** This theme covers the differentiable-physics series'
model of Primary substation demand as a function of the calendar and the weather, minus a solar
term: the convex model of part 2, its training-window and drift contrast, and the framework module
that the later parts of the series reuse. The [Experiments](experiments.md) page lists every
experiment tried, with its verdict and a take-home message, and the [Research plan](plan.md) lists
what is planned next.
