# Differentiable physics: demand, calendar, and weather: research plan

**Part 2 of the series, experiment 104, is the first experiment in this theme.** It builds the
framework module and asks whether a convex demand-minus-solar model gains from every usable year
with a slowly drifting level. The later parts of the series (pooling, long history and drift,
embedded generation, and the forecast engine) have their own themes, created in the pull request of
each part's first experiment.
